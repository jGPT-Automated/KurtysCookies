Our Story

A Tesla engineer
and his twin started
a cookie company.

"We knew our cookies were better than anything else in Austin — especially the big chains. So we started a company."

I'm Kurtis — Tesla engineer by day, serial baker by night. My twin brother Eric and I started Kurty's because we wanted to build something together. We'd been making chocolate chip cookies that, objectively, destroyed Crumbl, Insomnia, and Tiff's. So we started a company. We had so much fun that we haven't looked back.

Every batch is made at Prep ATX — a fully licensed commercial kitchen in Austin — with real butter, real eggs, and organic flour. No shortcuts. No preservatives. No artificial anything. Just cookies that are, genuinely, fucking amazing.
